Lee Carvallo's Putting Challenge

A golf game For ZX81 / Timex Sinclair 1000 computers with 16K RAM expansion pack.

Originally created for the Vintage Computer Festival West in Mountain View, CA in 2025. Inspired by Lee Carvallo's Putting Challenge from The Simpsons.
